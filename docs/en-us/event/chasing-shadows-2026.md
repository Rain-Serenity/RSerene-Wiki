## Prologue: Wind Sweeps Across 155, 81, -377
Sunlight pierces the clouds over the meadow at coordinates `(155, 81, -377)`. No enchanted powers, no modded effects — only the rustle of leather armor and the crisp thud of a slime ball striking the ground.
**Chasing Shadows** is a soccer tournament built for vanilla survival players: it doesn't test your gear, only your sprint speed, passing accuracy, and team coordination. Step onto the pitch and let speed be your language, with goals as your only echo.

## Core Mechanics
This tournament is built entirely on vanilla survival mechanics:
1. **Sulfur Block Ball Physics**: Uses the vanilla wool-filled sulfur block as the match ball, replicating soccer's bounce, roll, and collision feedback — no custom items needed
2. **Survival-Native Rules**: Cheats prohibited throughout; all actions follow standard vanilla survival constraints

## Entry Rules
### Eligibility
- Only players **without bans** on the survival or skyblock servers may register
- Register as a team of 4; <strong>fill in the <a href="https://docs.qq.com/sheet/DUkNOSU1mclV2Z2FD" style="color:#3498db;text-decoration:underline;">form</a></strong> to register
- Each player may join only **1** team

### Requirements
- Must wear **unenchanted leather armor** during matches — diamond/netherite armor, elytra, and Frost Walker boots are strictly prohibited
- Match balls are provided by the organizer before each match; **bringing your own items is forbidden**
- Players use **Knockback I sticks** or **body collisions** to control the sulfur block

### Prohibited
- Using flight, speed hacks, or **wall-clip cheats**
- **Maliciously damaging** the field, **interfering with referees**, or **harassing players**
- Intentionally **delaying the match** or using terrain to **trap the ball**
- Violations result in **immediate disqualification**; severe cases may lead to a ban

::: warning Gear Check
Referees will inspect all players' equipment before each match. Armor with Protection, Thorns, or Knockback enchantments will result in an automatic forfeit. Do not take the risk.
:::

## Tournament Format
1. **Knockout Stage**: Cross matches, randomly assigned; undefeated team is champion
2. **Ranking Stage**: Knockout losers continue playing to determine rankings

::: tip Ball Management
If the ball leaves the field, a linesman will throw it back in. Players are strictly forbidden from retrieving the ball themselves. If the ball disappears, the referee will pause the match and resume from the break point after deploying a new ball.
:::

::: warning Spectator Rules
Spectators must remain in the designated area. Entering the field or throwing eggs/snowballs to interfere with the match will result in being teleported out and temporarily losing spectator privileges.
:::
