## 🎯 Theme & Concept

**"RSerene Nine Provinces · Golden Autumn Bedwars" National Day Bed Wars Special** — During the National Day holiday, team-based Bed Wars battles take place on the RSerene event server. Players form squads, defend their own bed, break enemy beds, and eliminate opponents to compete for the Golden Autumn Bedwars championship.

Why do it? RSerene has always upheld a public-benefit, friendly, and fair community atmosphere. This National Day event does not change the survival server's original gameplay; instead, a Bed Wars mini-game is launched on a **standalone event server**, so both new and veteran players can join easily and enjoy teamwork, tactical bed-breaking, and National Day festivities.

**The Three Cores of Bed Wars:**

1. **Defend the bed**: The bed is your team's lifeline — while it stands, teammates can respawn; once destroyed, the whole team enters the elimination countdown
2. **Break beds**: Destroy enemy beds by bridging, ambushes, team fights, traps, and more — the key to victory
3. **Teamwork**: Resource sharing, defense and rotations, frontal pressure, and flanking attacks — none can be missed

## 🏆 Format & Scoring

**Match Mode**
- Group point matches + top-four finals
- Each match uses the official Bed Wars maps; exact player counts adjusted based on registrations
- Goal of each match: protect your team's bed, break enemy beds, eliminate all enemies

**Scoring Rules**

| Action             | Points                      |
|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| Win                | +5                          |
| Break an enemy bed | +2 / each                   |
| Kill               | +1 / each                   |
| Last one standing  | +1                          |
| Lose but broke a bed | Bed-break points still counted |

**Ranking Rules**
- Ranked by total points; ties broken in order: beds broken → kills → total survival time → head-to-head record
- The top four advance to the finals; the finals use a best-of-three format
- If time runs out without a winner, decided by remaining beds, kills, and beds broken
