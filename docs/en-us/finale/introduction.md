## Key Features

**Long-running, no hassle: no world resets and no frequent wipes; before switching modpacks we announce it in the group in advance and keep a backup of the old world.**

**Premium and offline both welcome: premium accounts log in directly; offline accounts join just as easily, so inviting friends has no barriers.**

**Vanilla pack experience, no private tweaks: the server runs the modpack's original mods list and adds no extra mods; if an adjustment is truly needed we explain it in the group first.**

**Small but stable: about 15 players online, no crowding; being unable to join when full is normal — wait a moment or say so in the group.**
