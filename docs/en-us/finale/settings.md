## Server Rules
- **No cheating**: hacks, x-ray, flight, auto-afk, scripted item farming and the like are all forbidden.
- **No malicious behaviour**: malicious PvP, base raiding, destroying others' builds, spam, insults.
- **Redstone & farms**: vanilla redstone contraptions and mob farms are allowed, but deliberately lagging the server or abusing chunk loaders is not.
- **Bug reporting**: report item-duplication/duping bugs privately to an admin; a reward is given after verification — do not exploit them yourself.
- **Resource usage**: this is a small server with limited bandwidth and memory; large-scale lagging behaviour will be asked to be fixed.
- **Maintenance**: there is no 24/7 on-call duty; restarts and maintenance are announced in the group.

> Specific terms are subject to the group announcement.
