# Server Rules

## I. Server Nature & Player Code of Conduct

### 1. Server Nature
- **Non-profit nature**: Rain Serenity is a non-profit Minecraft Java Edition server.
- **Donation principle**: This server contains no paid content of any kind, but accepts voluntary and unconditional donations from players.
- **Real-world trading disclaimer**: This server does not restrict players from trading in-game items for money in the real world, but bears no legal or joint liability for any real-world economic disputes arising between players.

### 2. Basic Player Code of Conduct
- **Age recommendation**: According to official regional ratings, this game is rated **7 and up** under the European PEGI system, **10 and up** under the North American ESRB system, and **8 years and up** in mainland China (CADPA) (the iOS App Store rating is 9+). If you are below the corresponding age, we recommend playing under the supervision of a guardian.
- **Player convention**: Please treat every player with respect and friendliness.
- **Account security and responsibility**: Players must keep their game accounts and passwords safe. Any item loss, ban or other consequence caused by lending, sharing or selling an account, or by a password leak, is borne by the player.

### 3. Dispute Resolution
- If you encounter problems, conflicts or disputes while playing, stay calm and rational, and contact an administrator for mediation when necessary.

## II. Service Boundaries & Disclaimer

### 1. Service Boundaries
- This is a non-profit server. The management team is **only responsible** for the basic operation of the server, rule maintenance and the handling of violations, and does **not provide** "nanny-style" personalised service.
- The management team is in principle not responsible for the following:
  1. **Builds and terrain**: does not help players move, migrate, copy, repair, rebuild or demolish buildings or building complexes; does not help break bedrock, clear terrain, or create or provide empty regions.
  2. **Accidents and losses**: does not provide compensation, rollback, reimbursement or restoration for item, build or terrain losses caused by player machine failures, redstone malfunctions, explosives, creepers, failed experiments or misoperation.
  3. **Player disputes**: does not intervene in in-game disputes arising from voluntary cooperation, borrowing, gifting, trading or shared facilities between players; however, violations such as cheating, theft, malicious destruction or malicious PvP are still handled according to the rules.

### 2. Risk Assumption & Violation Reporting
- Players should assess and bear the risks of building, redstone, explosives and experimental operations in game themselves.
- If a loss is caused by another player's violation, keep the evidence and report it through channels such as the QQ group; the management team will handle it according to the rules.

### 3. Management Team Assistance Notes
- Whether the management team provides additional assistance is decided by the management team itself; any assistance does not constitute an obligation, promise or precedent.
- Please do not harass, insult or spam the management team for failing to receive the above non-obligatory assistance; otherwise it will be handled according to provisions such as "inappropriate speech" and "malicious spam" in these rules.

## III. Violations & Handling

::: warning Violation Handling Notice
For the following violations, the management team will impose penalties such as warnings, temporary bans, permanent bans or removal from the community group chat, depending on the **severity** and the **actual impact** caused. If a violation involves multiple accounts, it will be treated as a violation by the main account.
:::

### 1. Community Behaviour Standards
- **Inappropriate speech**: discriminatory, offensive or seriously improper speech (see Section IV for details).
- **Harmful content**: publishing pornographic, disgusting or excessively violent content.
- **Malicious spam**: sending spam or maliciously spamming.
- **Impersonation & advertising**: impersonating others, publishing unpermitted advertisements or other improper behaviour.

### 2. In-Game Behaviour Standards
- **Cheating**
  - Using cheat clients, hacks or illegal mods.
  - Maliciously exploiting server or game vulnerabilities.
- **Malicious destruction**
  - Destroying or tampering with others' buildings and work without permission.
  - Taking or stealing items from others' containers without permission.
  - Malicious PvP (forcibly killing players in non-permitted areas or without mutual consent).
- **Mechanism abuse**
  - Abusing plugin features (such as using the claim plugin to fence off others' buildings).
  - Deliberately repeating the same command, or using macros/scripts to cause command spam.
- **Illegal buildings**
  - Building anything indecent, illegal or offensive.
- **Evading punishment**
  - Creating or using alt accounts (backup accounts) to evade bans, farm supplies or commit violations.

## IV. Zero-Tolerance Principle

::: danger Special Statement
Once verified, the following violations will result in an **immediate permanent ban and removal from the community group chat**, and **no appeal of any form will be accepted**.
:::

### 1. Speech & Privacy Red Lines

| Violation                            | Description                                                                                                   |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Racial & gender discrimination**   | Speech containing racial or gender discrimination, or attacks targeting a specific gender.                    |
| **LGBTQ+ discrimination**            | Discrimination, insult or hate speech against groups such as homosexual and transgender people.               |
| **Insulting vulnerable groups**      | Insults, mockery or hate speech against groups such as people with disabilities.                              |
| **Other hate speech**                | Any other form of hate speech not listed above.                                                               |
| **Privacy leaks**                    | Leaking, spreading or reselling others' real-world personal privacy or true information without permission.   |
| **Malicious personal attacks**       | Vicious personal attacks or toxic speech of a persistent harassing nature.                                    |

### 2. Major Serious Violations

| Violation                                   | Description                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|:--------|:------------|
| **Severely damaging the community**         | Repeatedly posting inappropriate speech, harmful content or malicious spam, seriously damaging the community atmosphere.                                                                     |
| **Severely damaging the game experience**   | Egregious cheating, malicious destruction, mechanism abuse or illegal buildings that seriously affect the normal operation of the server.                                                    |
| **Threatening server security**             | Behaviour endangering server security such as network attacks (e.g. DDoS) against the server, maliciously probing for vulnerabilities, or exploiting low-level vulnerabilities for profit.   |
| **Real-world illegal connections**          | Conducting or promoting real-world illegal or criminal activities in game or in the community group chat.                                                                                    |

## V. Ban Appeals & Supplementary Provisions

### 1. Appeal Process
- If you object to a ban that does not fall under the "**Zero-Tolerance Principle**", you may submit an appeal to an administrator through channels such as the server QQ group within **7 days** of receiving the ban notice, requesting a reduced or lifted ban. Missing the deadline is deemed acceptance of the penalty.
- Bans for violating any provision of Section IV "Zero-Tolerance Principle" of these rules will no longer be accepted for appeal.

### 2. Supplementary Provisions
- **Management team statement**: All management team members are players who participate in server management on a voluntary basis; please respect the administrators' work. If you object to an administrator's decision, please resolve it through proper channels (such as an appeal); personal attacks, insults or spamming against administrators in the group or in game are strictly prohibited.
- **Final right of interpretation**: The management team reserves the final right of interpretation and revision of these server rules.
- **Rule revisions**: These rules will be revised from time to time according to the actual operation of the server. New rules take effect immediately upon release; in case of conflict, the latest version prevails.
