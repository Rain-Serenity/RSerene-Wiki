## I. Login & Joining
### Q1: Do I need a premium account?
A: Although the server does not have premium authentication enabled, non-premium users need <a href="https://littleskin.cn" target="_blank">LittleSkin</a> login to join the server. Therefore, non-premium users, please register an account at <a href="https://littleskin.cn" target="_blank">LittleSkin</a> and log in with your <a href="https://littleskin.cn" target="_blank">LittleSkin</a> account in a third-party launcher before joining the server!

### Q2: Where do I download the modpack?
A: From the **group files** of QQ group 902620497; you can also download it yourself from the [original author's release page](https://www.mcmod.cn/modpack/1133.html).

### Q3: How do I join the server?
A: 1. Join QQ group 902620497 and download the "Closing Song" modpack from the **group files**, or download it from the [official Closing Song page](https://www.mcmod.cn/modpack/1133.html).<br>
1. Use PCL, HMCL or another launcher to "import the modpack" directly — do not reinstall or add/remove mods;<br>
2. Multiplayer → Add server, address `CLSO.RSerene.com`;<br>
3. On your first join you can ask an admin for starter supplies; see the actual server status for details.

### Q4: Why can't I connect / why do I keep reconnecting?
A: First confirm you are using the correct modpack and that the mods match the pack; the address is `CLSO.RSerene.com`.

### Q5: Does it cost money?
A: No, playing is free (donations are entirely voluntary, never required).

## II. Server Mechanics & Rules
### Q1: How many players can the server hold?
A: About 15 players online at once. When full you cannot join — wait a moment or ask in the group.

### Q2: Will the world be reset?
A: No. The old world is backed up before any modpack change.

### Q3: When will the modpack change?
A: Once we have played this one through; before switching there is a vote/announcement in the group.

### Q4: What should I do about lag or disconnects?
A: Check the group announcement for restart schedules first; if many people disconnect at once it is usually a server restart or network fluctuation — wait a moment and rejoin.

### Q5: What are the server rules?
A: No hacks, x-ray, flight, auto-afk or scripted item farming; no malicious PvP, base raiding, destroying others' builds, spam or insults; vanilla redstone contraptions and mob farms are allowed, but deliberately lagging the server or abusing chunk loaders is not. Specific terms are subject to the [Server Rules](/en-us/about/rules.html).

## III. Copyright & Community
### Q1: May I redistribute or sell the modpack?
A: No. This server charges no fees and does not sell or commercially distribute the modpack in any form; it is only for normal play by players of this server.

### Q2: Where do I report problems?
A: Ask an online admin in-game, or join the official QQ group 902620497 and @Chosen_1st or @Korvahet in the group.
