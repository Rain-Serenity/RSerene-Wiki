## Key Features

**Full Slimefun industry loop: start at research levels, move through the tool workshop, alchemical evolution and mineral processing — a 38-tier research ladder runs through the entire progression line.**

**RPG skill growth: mcMMO provides 19 trainable skills, capped at level 1000, with no level loss on death and shared experience when you party up.**

**Interstellar exploration: ClayTech comes with rockets and spacesuits, so you can travel from the Overworld to the Moon, Mars and the asteroid belt — and build your own base on a planet.**

**Claim protection: up to 8 claims (12 during events), each one up to 512×512×256 — select an area with a wooden hoe and hold string to view claim info.**

**Economy and shops: CMI has a built-in currency and item price table, so you can check values, sell items and transfer money to other players.**

**Friendly to new and returning players: you get a Slimefun starter guide on your first join, can look up recipes in-game at any time, and the server even includes a recipe calculator so you never have to count materials by hand.**
