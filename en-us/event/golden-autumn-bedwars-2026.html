🎯 Theme & Concept

"RSerene Nine Provinces · Golden Autumn Bedwars" National Day Bed Wars Special — During the National Day holiday, team-based Bed Wars battles take place on the RSerene event server. Players form squads, defend their own bed, break enemy beds, and eliminate opponents to compete for the Golden Autumn Bedwars championship.

Why do it? RSerene has always upheld a public-benefit, friendly, and fair community atmosphere. This National Day event does not change the survival server's original gameplay; instead, a Bed Wars mini-game is launched on a standalone event server, so both new and veteran players can join easily and enjoy teamwork, tactical bed-breaking, and National Day festivities.

The Three Cores of Bed Wars:

  1. Defend the bed: The bed is your team's lifeline — while it stands, teammates can respawn; once destroyed, the whole team enters the elimination countdown
  2. Break beds: Destroy enemy beds by bridging, ambushes, team fights, traps, and more — the key to victory
  3. Teamwork: Resource sharing, defense and rotations, frontal pressure, and flanking attacks — none can be missed

📋 Entry Rules

Eligibility

  • All players on the RSerene server may participate
  • Solo or team registration supported; team size 2 — 4 players
  • Fill in the registration formopen in new window to register
  • Teams with fewer than 4 players can be matched with teammates randomly

Warning

Failing to show up without reason during the expected match times after registration, withdrawing late, or not meeting entry requirements will result in a deduction of the registration fee 999.99 and will affect eligibility for future events.

Hard Entry Requirements

  1. Keep the client vanilla: Only the server's official Bed Wars gameplay is allowed. Hacks, x-ray, flying, auto-clickers, macros, packet modification, and cheat resource packs are prohibited
  2. Follow referee instructions: Enter the event server at the designated time, and join the designated map and team room
  3. Fixed teams: No unauthorized player swaps during the match; special cases require advance application to the referees
  4. No cross-team cooperation: No throwing, giving away resources, malicious teaming, or playing on someone else's behalf
  5. No malicious exploitation of bugs: Including clipping through walls, resource farming, item duplication, glitching out of the map, and deliberately delaying the match
  6. Sportsmanship: Insults, harassment, and malicious disruption of other contestants are prohibited

Prohibited

  • Using hacks, cheat Mods, sound-altering resource packs, or auto-clicking tools
  • Copying others' tactics while maliciously claiming them as original
  • Maliciously disrupting other contestants' matches
  • Violating any of the above will result in immediate disqualification and action per the Server Rules

🏆 Format & Scoring

Match Mode

  • Group point matches + top-four finals
  • Each match uses the official Bed Wars maps; exact player counts adjusted based on registrations
  • Goal of each match: protect your team's bed, break enemy beds, eliminate all enemies

Scoring Rules

ActionPoints
Win+5
Break an enemy bed+2 / each
Kill+1 / each
Final kill+1 / each

Ranking Rules

  • Ranked by total points; ties broken in order: beds broken → kills → total survival time → head-to-head record
  • The top four advance to the finals; the finals use a best-of-three format
  • If time runs out without a winner, decided by remaining beds, kills, and beds broken
