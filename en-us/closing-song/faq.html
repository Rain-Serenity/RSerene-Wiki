I. Login & Joining

Q1: Do I need a premium account?

A: No. Both premium and offline accounts can join directly, with no whitelist application.

Q2: Where do I download the modpack?

A: From the group files of QQ group 902620497. Use the original pack and do not add or remove mods; you can also search the internet for the original author's release page.

Q3: How do I join the server?

A: 1. Join QQ group 902620497 and download the "Closing Song" modpack from the group files;
2. Use PCL, HMCL or another launcher to "import the modpack" directly — do not reinstall or add/remove mods;
3. After launching, confirm the version is 1.20.1-Forge and that you are running Java 17;
4. Multiplayer → Add server, address CLSO.RSerene.com;
5. On your first join you can ask an admin for starter supplies; see the group announcement for details.

Q4: Why can't I connect / why do I keep reconnecting?

A: First confirm the version is 1.20.1-Forge, that you are running Java 17, and that the mods match the pack; the address is CLSO.RSerene.com. If it still fails, ask in the group — the address may have changed.

Q5: Does it cost money?

A: No, playing is free (donations are entirely voluntary, never required).

II. Server Mechanics & Rules

Q1: How many players can the server hold?

A: About 15 players online at once. When full you cannot join — wait a moment or ask in the group.

Q2: Will the world be reset?

A: No. The old world is backed up before any modpack change.

Q3: When will the modpack change?

A: Once we have played this one through; before switching there is a vote/announcement in the group.

Q4: What should I do about lag or disconnects?

A: Check the group announcement for restart schedules first; if many people disconnect at once it is usually a server restart or network fluctuation — wait a moment and rejoin.

Q5: What are the server rules?

A: No hacks, x-ray, flight, auto-afk or scripted item farming; no malicious PvP, base raiding, destroying others' builds, spam or insults; vanilla redstone contraptions and mob farms are allowed, but deliberately lagging the server or abusing chunk loaders is not. Specific terms are subject to the group announcement.

III. Copyright & Community

Q1: May I redistribute or sell the modpack?

A: No. This server charges no fees and does not sell or commercially distribute the modpack in any form; it is only for normal play by players of this server.

Q2: Where do I report problems?

A: Ask an online admin in-game, or join the official QQ group 902620497 and @admin or @Chosen-1st in the group.
